3. 主要问题（Major Weaknesses，按录用影响排序）
M01 [P0 / 已确认] 新增主张、基线、指标和结论之间出现系统性的“结果缺席”
证据链：§3.9 定义整会议在线运行 [p.5–6, L406–434]；§4 增加 turn-trained/oracle/contextual/post-hoc 和多个在线
基线 [p.7, L525–566]；Metrics 定义 tcpBLEU、整会议听众端 lag、双 speaker-verification [p.7, L567–608]；Statistics
说两侧检验、Holm 和 extra seeds [p.7, L608–617]。但实际 Table 1–15 仍只覆盖原有的参考窗口评测、旧语音客观分数
及旧的 bootstrap；并无“新完整会议汇总表/图”、新基线分数、额外 seed 平均/方差、TitaNet 结果、tcpBLEU 数值
或尾部实际延迟直方图。尤其 Conclusion 完全未讨论任何新增整会议测量。[pp.8–9, L736–743]
为何关键：读者不应通过作者“计划测量/写了指标定义”的文字来推定其已完成了实验。新增这些内容还改变了论文的
论证结构：如果整会议实验表现不佳，系统最强应用结论会进一步缩小；如果表现好，当前稿却没有给出可以复核的数
字。
必须补：至少一张完整会议文本+语音主表；一张公平基线对照表；一张统计与跨种子表；一段关于真实 Mandarin 结果
的限制性讨论。每个新 baseline 必须在一张明确的表中有实际指标，或者删去“已运行”暗示、标记为拟议未来工作。
不要把所有新增实验藏在仅口头引用的附录。
建议作者回答：这些数值是尚未跑完、跑完但 make_tables_v6.py  未写入 PDF，还是意图另附 supplement？当前附件不
允许审稿人区分。
M02 [P0 / 已确认 + 证据缺失] §3.9 是完整会议的算法草图，不是完整会议评估；Limitations 直接否定
新主张
证据：§3.9 规定 diarizer>0.5 启动窗口，连续 1s 无活动关闭，最多 20s，前后 padding，decoder 在窗口边界重置，而
speaker slots 延续；音频播放队列跨窗口累计，计算时间计入 commit。[v6 pp.5–6, L412–434]。但是 §4 的 
the whole-meeting evaluation (§??)  没有真实定位 [p.6, L458–459]；Metrics 的 tcpBLEU、listener-side lag 无结果
[p.7]；Limitations 原文仍陈述 we have not scored whole meetings end to end ，以及 listener-side lag is measured within
windows  [p.10, L760–773]。
评价：这是可直接证实的文本与证据矛盾，不能被视为“作者没有实现整会议系统”的直接证明。算法写出来不等于做
了测试。与原来的 reference-utterance windows 不同，整会议覆盖全部听到的语音，并涉及未标注/未过滤的
backchannel、连续超过 20s 的流和长时间 TTS backlog。没有这部分对照就无法评价核心应用是否成立。
最小可接受实验：选定 AMI 官方完整 16 个测试 meeting + AliMeeting 完整 20 个 meeting，按时间推进一次，不使用参
考分割决定窗口或 <eos> 。明确音频重放条件、silence threshold、speaker cache、每次 read 的 x≤t 、每次 TTS 能否
及时播完；报告 full-meeting tcpBLEU 、归属错率、coverage、遗漏/重译、输出时长比、speaker identity stability、文
本首字及音频首包/播放完成 lag、p50/p90/p95 backlog。务必与相同 online windowing 和同一个 Talker 的强基线比
较。
附加安全检查：对 20s 硬切时正在说话的 utterance 是否被截断、最终末读 flush 的构造、0.2s 前置填充需要多少历史缓
存、0.3s 尾部填充如何延迟关闭，都需可执行伪代码。仅“窗口已被某规则切出”并不能让这些时间戳在线可用。

M05 [P0 / 已确认] 双侧统计/多重校正的陈述与 Table 9、Appendix F 的算法定义互相冲突
原文：§4 宣称使用 meeting paired bootstrap、permutation test、leave-one-meeting-out，p values are two-
sided 、pre-registered tests ... Holm-corrected  [v6 p.7, L608–617]。Appendix F 却仍说 session speaker matching 在
全测试集上固定，按 window/meeting 做 bootstrap [p.13, L1067–1074]；Table 9 caption 则把 p 定义成 the share of
resamples in which the first system is not better  [p.15, Table 9]。这个定义是单侧尾部比例，不是普通的 two-sided
p，更不是已展示的 Holm-adjusted permutation p。
可核查数值实例：Table 9 中 En→Zh MIST-Simul-M, Δ=2  相比 MIST-Simul, Δ=2 ，window 95% CI 为 [-0.10, 7.60] 却列
p=0.043，meeting CI [-0.06, 6.90] 却列 p=0.034。Zh→En 语音 MIST-Simul vs MIST+LA2 ，window CI [-0.09, 4.09] 却

Appendix A — Ready-to-adapt English ACL/ARR-style review
Summary
This submission studies speaker-attributed simultaneous speech-to-speech meeting interpretation. It combines
frozen Qwen3-Omni acoustic representations with the activity posteriors of a frozen streaming Sortformer, inserts
learned speaker-activity tokens among acoustic tokens, and trains a unified Thinker decoder to emit speaker-
attributed, timestamped translations and a wait  token. A separately trained UniSS Talker converts translated
segments into voice-prompted speech. The paper reports substantial speaker-attributed text translation gains
over a same-data diarization-then-translation cascade on reference-segmented AMI windows (+6.52 cpBLEU for
En→Zh and +3.50 for revoiced Zh→En). Its learned commit policy substantially outperforms LA2 applied to the
offline model on En→Zh, but not Zh→En. On real Mandarin AliMeeting, the offline model does not outperform the
same-data cascade.
Strengths
The problem is timely and practically relevant: an interpreter needs to convey speaker identity, translation
content, and approximate timing under overlapping speech.
The division of labor between an external long-term speaker tracker and a speech LLM capable of short-
window translation is a plausible and interesting system design.
The format that jointly emits speaker, timestamps, target-language text, and wait  is technically specific and
potentially useful beyond the present application.
The same-data cascade comparison, session-level speaker-aware evaluation, and extensive AMI analysis
provide informative evidence that the approach is effective under the reported reference-window conditions.
This revision improves the framing by changing the title to “Diarizer-Conditioned” and explicitly describing
the separate frozen diarizer/encoder and externally trained Talker. It also discloses strong data-selection
limitations and correctly notes that both AMI directions use the same underlying meetings.
Major weaknesses
W1. The new evaluation protocol and the reported results are inconsistent. The revision introduces a whole-
meeting online-window algorithm (§3.9), complete-meeting tcpBLEU  and listener-side playback lag, stronger
cascade/online baselines, extra En→Zh seeds, and two-sided permutation/Holm-corrected tests (§4). However,
none of the new whole-meeting results, new baseline scores, seed summaries, or time-constrained metric scores
appears in the presented tables. The Limitations section still explicitly states that whole meetings have not been
evaluated end to end and that listener-side lag is measured within windows. Consequently, the strongest new
claims cannot be evaluated from the submitted PDF.
W2. The key comparisons remain confounded. The +10.99 En→Zh simultaneous gain is relative to LA2 on a
model trained on complete windows, which the paper itself reports as degenerate under partial input. The
revision now defines LA2 on the prefix-trained model, fixed-lag controls, and a streaming cascade, but provides no
results. Similarly, a same-data translator that is trained on multi-speaker windows and applied to diarization turns
is not equivalent to a turn-trained cascade; the newly described turn-trained, contextual, and oracle-turn
baselines are not scored. The newly described post-hoc relabelling baseline is essential for determining whether
interleaving is better than supplying the same persistent diarizer identities after decoding, but it is also missing.
W3. The new statistical claims do not match the appendix. The experimental setup states that p values are two-
sided and selected tests are Holm-corrected. Appendix F and Table 9 still describe a bootstrap tail probability
defined as the fraction of resamples in which the first system is not better. Some reported p values below 0.05 are
accompanied by 95% confidence intervals that include zero, which is consistent with mixing one-sided tail

Recommendation
Weak Reject; confidence 4/5 (simulated). The technical direction and the reported reference-window AMI gains
are promising, and the revision identifies several important evaluation deficiencies. Nevertheless, the submitted
PDF contains new proposed evaluation machinery without the corresponding empirical results, several explicit
contradictions between setup and limitations, and unresolved cross-references. The current evidence does not yet

substantially more favorable if the authors supplied the new matched baselines, continuous-meeting results and
