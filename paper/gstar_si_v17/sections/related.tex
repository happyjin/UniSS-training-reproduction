\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Simultaneous speech translation.} Wait-$k$~\citep{ma2019stacl} writes after a fixed delay;
LocalAgreement~\citep{liu2020low,polak2022cuni,machacek2023turning} commits the prefix two consecutive hypotheses agree
on; learned policies emit a wait token~\citep{koshkin2024transllama} or read the decoder's attention to the
audio~\citep{papi2023edatt,papi2024streamatt}; latency is measured by LAAL~\citep{papi2022over}. SimulS2ST systems
keep one speaker's voice~\citep{zhang2024streamspeech,seamless2023,labiausse2025hibiki,cheng2025liveinterpret,he2026simuls2st,deng2025simuls2s}.

\paragraph{Latent timing and structured prediction.} Monotonic attention (MILk, MMA and EMMA, the policy of
SeamlessStreaming;~\citealp{arivazhagan2019milk,ma2020mma,ma2023emma}) and the Hidden Markov
Transformer~\citep{zhang2023hmt} treat how much source is read before a target token is written as latent and train it
by the likelihood of the target; Hibiki places each target word after the source that makes it
predictable~\citep{labiausse2025hibiki}. All of them time the \emph{text}. Playback alignment times the \emph{speech} of
fixed text: its state is the queue of synthesised speech, its transitions are deterministic, its potentials score
renderings by intelligibility and heard delay, and it trains the speech generator, not the translation policy. It is a
linear-chain CRF~\citep{lafferty2001crf} with a posterior-regularised latency budget~\citep{ganchev2010posterior};
automatic dubbing instead controls the length of the translation~\citep{lakew2022isometric}.

\paragraph{Multi-speaker recognition and translation.} Serialised output training transcribes overlapping speakers
in order~\citep{kanda2020sot,kanda2021saasr}, also in streaming~\citep{kanda2022tsot} and for joint recognition and
translation~\citep{papi2023tsot}; streaming diarizers keep speaker order over long
recordings~\citep{park2024sortformer,medennikov2025streaming,medennikov2026nemotron}, and diarization can condition a
recogniser~\citep{polok2026dicow,huo2026tagspeech} or be interleaved with a speech LLM's audio tokens
(G-STAR;~\citealp{peng2026gstar}). STAC-ST translates two-party calls offline~\citep{zuluaga2023stac};
DiariST~\citep{yang2024diarist} and X-Translator~\citep{zhao2026xtranslator} attribute streaming text translation to
speakers. Voice-cloned translation lowers listeners' workload in online meetings~\citep{ma2026samevoice}.
