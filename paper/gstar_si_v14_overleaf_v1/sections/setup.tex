\paragraph{Data.} \emph{En$\rightarrow$Zh}: AMI~\citep{carletta2005ami} IHM-mix recordings (headsets summed into one
channel), official split; test references are machine translations selected by quality estimation
(Appendix~\ref{app:data}); 16 test
meetings, 35\% of whose reference windows hold several speakers. \emph{Zh$\rightarrow$En}: the Chinese translations
re-spoken in each speaker's voice at the original times, with the human AMI transcripts as references (a synthetic
source). \emph{Real Mandarin}: the AliMeeting test
set~\citep{yu2022m2met}, 20 meetings of two to four people (10.8\,h), headset channels mixed into one; no AliMeeting
data is used in training, and it is scored against DiariST's human English translations~\citep{yang2024diarist}.\ifAliEval{} The eight AliMeeting
\emph{evaluation} meetings (\num{v11:ae:hours}\,h) are used only for the locked test of \S\ref{sec:res-alieval}.\fi

\paragraph{Models.} Encoder and Thinker are Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct~\citep{xu2025qwen3omni}; the diarizer is
Streaming Sortformer~\citep{medennikov2025streaming}. The deadline Thinker is trained on both directions on 8 H200 GPUs
and used at its last step (Appendix~\ref{app:train}). The base Talker is a UniSS model~\citep{uniss2025} fine-tuned for
block-wise streaming speech; the PA Talker is fine-tuned from it once per direction on candidates rendered for 15 AMI
training meetings, whose text came from separately trained single-direction Thinkers, not from the deadline Thinker
(Appendix~\ref{app:plem}). Decoding is greedy.

\paragraph{Systems.} Table~\ref{tab:main} compares multi-speaker speech-to-speech systems on the same online detector
windows. \emph{SeamlessStreaming}~\citep{seamless2023} is a public single-speaker SimulS2ST model, run zero-shot.\ifXT{} \emph{X-Translator}~\citep{zhao2026xtranslator} is an open, speaker-aware cascade (streaming
ASR, MT and voice-cloning TTS with a voice prompt per tracked speaker), run zero-shot with its released defaults (input level set on dev for AMI; Appendix~\ref{app:xt}).\fi\ifCascade{} The
\emph{cascade} is a speaker-blind streaming model trained on our data (\sys{}-Simul without speaker tokens), spoken by
the base Talker (Appendix~\ref{app:cascade}).\fi{} Lacking meeting-wide speaker labels, these
systems are attributed causally by the diarizer slot most active over each segment (Appendix~\ref{app:e7}). \emph{B} is our deadline Thinker with the
base Talker (fixed rate, one sample per block, no merging); \sys{} is the same Thinker and text with the playback layer,
whose components Table~\ref{tab:ablation} adds to B one at a time.

\paragraph{Metrics.} Text is scored by \textbf{tcpBLEU}, the translation analogue of tcpWER~\citep{neumann2023meeteval}:
output slots are matched to reference speakers per meeting by chrF~\citep{popovic2015chrf}, and each output segment
joins the reference unit of its matched speaker that it overlaps within a 2\,s collar (Appendix~\ref{app:metrics}).
Speech is transcribed by Whisper-large-v3~\citep{radford2023robust} (English) or Paraformer~\citep{gao2022paraformer}
(Chinese) and scored, averaged over TTS runs, by two speaker-attributed BLEU scores: \textbf{cpASR-BLEU}, the
meeting-level analogue of cpWER~\citep{watanabe2020chime6}, concatenates each speaker's output and reference over the
meeting, matches speakers one-to-one by chrF and scores the pairs (unmatched sides against empty strings), so a sentence
given to the wrong speaker costs precision and recall; \textbf{tcpASR-BLEU} adds tcpBLEU's time constraint. BLEU is corpus-level sacreBLEU~\citep{post2018call}
(13a for English, zh for Chinese)\ifMetricsV{}; COMET~\citep{rei2022comet} and a second recogniser are in
Appendix~\ref{app:metricsv11}\fi. \textbf{Latency} is measured per speaker stream from audio and playback time, without
computation: conventional speech LAAL~\citep{papi2022over} charges every word of a block when the block starts playing
(\emph{LAAL}, or LAAL$_\text{block}$); \emph{EndOffset} runs from a unit's end to the end of its last heard word; speech
start and LAAL$_\text{spread}$, which spreads a block's words over its playback as Eq.~\eqref{eq:lat} does, are in
Appendix~\ref{app:chunk}.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Paired tests are over meetings (exact sign-flip test, 95\% meeting bootstrap, both TTS seeds
pooled). Confirmatory contrasts were fixed in internal analysis plans before test decoding (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg})
and Holm-corrected: \sys{} $-$ B over twelve BLEU tests (Table~\ref{tab:sig}), and \sys{} $-$ each system of
Table~\ref{tab:main} per BLEU score, fixed before the added systems were decoded; ablation differences are
exploratory.
