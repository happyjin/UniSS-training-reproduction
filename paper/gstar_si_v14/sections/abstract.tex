\begin{abstract}
A meeting interpreter must say what each person said, attribute it to the right person, and get the translation to the
listener in time, including when several people talk at once. In \sys{}, a speech LLM reads acoustic tokens interleaved
with tokens of a frozen streaming diarizer and writes speaker-first, time-stamped translations for every speaker of an
overlapping window; a speech generator speaks them in each speaker's voice. Online, much of the delay a listener
experiences arises \emph{after} the text is written, while synthesised speech queues behind earlier speech of the same
speaker. We cast this as structured prediction: \emph{playback alignment} is a linear-chain CRF whose states are the
speaker's playback backlog and candidate renderings of each block, with potentials for the generator's likelihood, the
fidelity of an independent recogniser and the delay at which every word is heard. Exact forward--backward inference
sums over all rendering sequences, a latency budget is imposed by posterior regularisation, whose dual we solve in
closed form up to a monotone one-dimensional search, and the CRF posterior is distilled into the speech generator. On
fixed text, on whole AMI meetings (Zh$\rightarrow$En / En$\rightarrow$Zh) and real Mandarin AliMeeting meetings, the
playback layer raises speaker-attributed speech BLEU (tcpASR-BLEU) by \num{v10:sig:test:ami:speech:e2-b:diff} /
\num{v10:sig:test:en:speech:e2-b:diff} / \num{v10:sig:test:ali:speech:e2-b:diff}, lowers conventional block-start
speech LAAL by \num{v10:pr:test:ami:e2-b:bslaal:diffabs} / \num{v10:pr:test:en:e2-b:bslaal:diffabs} /
\num{v10:pr:test:ali:e2-b:bslaal:diffabs}\,s and ends each utterance's speech
\num{v10:pr:test:ami:e2-b:endoff:diffabs} / \num{v10:pr:test:en:e2-b:endoff:diffabs} /
\num{v10:pr:test:ali:e2-b:endoff:diffabs}\,s earlier (all significant). Adding its components one at a time, the backlog-dependent rate, the
CRF-distilled speech generator and selection by recogniser fidelity each add to the gain, which is largest where queues
are longest.\ifAliEval{} \num{v11:ae:abstract}\fi
\end{abstract}
