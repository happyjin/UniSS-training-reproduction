\subsection{When the translation is heard}
\label{sec:res-pa}
\label{sec:res-v10}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} gives the deadline baseline B and the components of \sys{}'s playback layer added one at a
time, all on the deadline Thinker's text, so differences come from the speech layer alone.

\paragraph{Against the deadline baseline.} Besides the speech-quality gain of Table~\ref{tab:main} (all
$p_\text{Holm}<0.001$, Table~\ref{tab:sig}), \sys{} lowers LAAL$_\text{block}$ by
\num{v10:pr:test:ami:e2-b:bslaal:diffabs} / \num{v10:pr:test:en:e2-b:bslaal:diffabs} /
\num{v10:pr:test:ali:e2-b:bslaal:diffabs}\,s and EndOffset by \num{v10:pr:test:ami:e2-b:endoff:diffabs} /
\num{v10:pr:test:en:e2-b:endoff:diffabs} / \num{v10:pr:test:ali:e2-b:endoff:diffabs}\,s (all $p<0.001$,
Table~\ref{tab:speech}); speech start barely moves, as the playback layer acts after the first block is written.

\paragraph{What each component adds.} The rows of Table~\ref{tab:ablation} follow the terms of Eq.~\eqref{eq:plpot}.
Merging (R1) changes little; the backlog-dependent base rate (R2) gives the largest step, +1.55 / +0.24 / +0.80
tcpASR-BLEU; the CRF-distilled PA Talker (R3) adds +0.29 / +0.02 / +0.33 (exploratory $p$ 0.002 / 0.82 / 0.001) and
ends speech 0.18 / 0.15 / 0.23\,s earlier, because the delay price, propagated by the backward messages of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:w}, favours renderings that keep the queue short: the wait behind earlier speech falls from 0.65 to
0.14\,s per unit in AMI Zh$\rightarrow$En and from 1.33 to 0.33\,s on AliMeeting (Table~\ref{tab:decomp}). Selecting
by the fidelity term $f$ at test time (R4) adds +0.18 / +0.30 / +0.27.
