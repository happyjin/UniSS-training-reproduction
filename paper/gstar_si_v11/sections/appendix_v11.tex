\section{The Selection Rule's Checkpoint}
\label{app:s4000}
The stage-2 checkpoint of \sys{}+WA reported in v10 (step 3{,}500) was not the one the pre-set selection rule picks
(step 4{,}000). We registered the comparison and decoded step 4{,}000 once on every test set with the same bias
($\bias=-1$) and playback layer (Table~\ref{tab:s4000}). It gives \num{v11:s4000:summary}; neither checkpoint improves
on \sys{} in ASR-BLEU, and both are heard later. The conclusion of \S\ref{sec:write} does not depend on the choice.

\section{Locked Test on Unused AliMeeting Meetings}
\label{app:alieval}
The eight AliMeeting evaluation meetings (\num{v11:ae:hours}\,h of meeting audio) were never used for training,
development or any earlier test. Windows, diarizer runs and scoring follow the AliMeeting test set, with two
differences fixed before decoding: the references are the human English translations of DiariST (no machine
translation), and no window is filtered by its reference. The analysis plan fixed four systems (B, \sys{} and
\sys{}+WA at steps 3{,}500 and 4{,}000) and three confirmatory hypotheses (\sys{} $-$ B on ASR-BLEU, LAAL$_\text{block}$
and EndOffset, Holm over three); everything else is exploratory. \num{v11:ae:wa}
