\section{Factorial Ablation of the Playback Layer}
\label{app:factorial}
Rows R1--R5 add one component at a time on B's text (\S\ref{sec:res-pa}); R3$^\prime$ is R3 with the rule's rate
shift raised until its mean development rate matches the PA Talker's (shift chosen on development meetings before
test decoding). The contrast fixed in the analysis plan is \sys{} (R5) against R3$^\prime$ on ASR-BLEU and LAAL$_\text{block}$ on
the three test sets, Holm over six; all other contrasts are descriptive. The backlog rate rule asks each block for
the rate of its source pace plus three rate steps per second of backlog (at most six), shifted by a constant number of
steps (two in R2 and R3, eight in R3$^\prime$); R4 replaces the base Talker by the PA Talker under the same rule with
one sample, so that R4 $-$ R2 isolates the learned Talker and R5 $-$ R4 CTC selection.

\ifFluencyPA
\section{A Speaking-Time Budget for the PA Talker}
\label{app:fluencypa}
The PA Talker speaks faster than B and pauses about twice as often (\S\ref{sec:res-pa}). PA-F adds to the posterior
regularisation of \S\ref{sec:method-plem} a second expected constraint, a lower bound on speech duration per output word,
$\mathbb{E}_q[\sum_i D(c_i)]/\sum_iW_i\ge s_{\min}$. The projection stays a linear-chain CRF, with $+\mu D(c)$ added to
Eq.~\eqref{eq:plpot} and $\lambda,\mu\ge0$ found by alternating bisection (Zh$\rightarrow$En $\lambda^\star=3.14$,
$\mu^\star=16.4$; En$\rightarrow$Zh $4.49$, $27.6$). The bound halves the PA Talker's speed-up on the training
candidates, $s_{\min}=1/(r_0+\tfrac12(r_\text{PA}-r_0))$ with $r_0$ the base-speed rate and $r_\text{PA}$ the PA
posterior's expected rate; training, checkpoint and decoding are those of the PA Talker. One setting was run, with the
gate of earlier stages, both fixed before training (Table~\ref{tab:fluencypa}). \num{v11:flu:app} PA-F pauses less and
speaks more slowly, but the fluency gain is not free in recognisability, so \sys{} keeps the PA Talker.

\ifWallclock
\section{Computation-Aware Replay}
\label{app:wallclock}
All latencies in the main text exclude computation. To measure it for the final systems, we decoded a fixed set of
whole test meetings again with the test settings, timing every read of the Thinker (prefix re-encoding included, GPU
synchronised) and every Talker generation and candidate-scoring call, each job alone on one H200 GPU (a job was
repeated, up to three times, when another process appeared on its GPU). A replay then schedules the reads on one
decoder stream per meeting, each read starting when it is due or when the previous one ends, and the blocks on one
Talker worker per meeting in release order, and plays them as in the main evaluation (Table~\ref{tab:wallclock}).
