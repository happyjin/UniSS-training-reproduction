\paragraph{Data.} \emph{En$\rightarrow$Zh}: AMI~\citep{carletta2005ami} IHM-mix recordings (headsets summed into one
channel), official split; each utterance's translation is selected from three MT systems by reference-free quality
estimation and an audio check, so test references are machine translations (Appendix~\ref{app:data}). Reference
windows group overlapping or touching utterances (at most 20\,s): 2{,}375 test windows from 16 meetings, 35\% with
several speakers; whole-meeting runs read all test audio. \emph{Zh$\rightarrow$En}: the Chinese translations
re-spoken in each speaker's voice at the original times, with the human AMI transcripts as references (a synthetic
source). \emph{Real Mandarin}: the AliMeeting test
set~\citep{yu2022m2met}, 20 meetings of two to four people (10.8\,h), headset channels mixed into one; no AliMeeting
data is used in training, and it is scored against DiariST's human English translations~\citep{yang2024diarist} (the
overlap study of \S\ref{sec:res-overlap} uses machine translations).\ifAliEval{} The eight AliMeeting
\emph{evaluation} meetings (\num{v11:ae:hours}\,h) are used only for the locked test of \S\ref{sec:res-alieval}.\fi

\paragraph{Models.} Encoder and Thinker are Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct~\citep{xu2025qwen3omni}; the diarizer is
Streaming Sortformer~\citep{medennikov2025streaming} (offline and low-latency presets). \sys{} (offline) and the
streaming Thinker are each one bidirectional model; the latter is trained with the deadline objective from \sys{}
(offline) on \num{v10:c:j:rows} rows (batches of 64, 32 rows per direction) for \num{v10:c:j:s1steps} steps on 8 H200
GPUs and used at its last step (Appendix~\ref{app:train}). The base Talker is a UniSS model~\citep{uniss2025}
fine-tuned for block-wise streaming speech. The PA Talker is fine-tuned from it once per direction (1{,}200 steps
Zh$\rightarrow$En) on candidates rendered for 15 AMI training meetings; their text came from separately trained
single-direction streaming Thinkers, not from the deadline Thinker whose text it speaks at test time
(Appendix~\ref{app:plem}). Decoding is greedy.

\paragraph{Systems.} \emph{B}: the deadline Thinker with the base Talker (fixed rate, one sample per block, no
merging). \emph{\sys{}}: the same Thinker and text with the playback layer. \emph{Rule-based layers} (R1--R3$^\prime$,
Table~\ref{tab:main}) add read-level merging, a backlog-dependent speaking rate and best-of-two CTC selection to B one
at a time, without the CRF; R3$^\prime$ is the strictest control, whose rate is matched to \sys{}'s on development
meetings. For attribution, a \emph{cascade} translates the diarizer's turns with our speaker-blind window model, and
\emph{relabelling} gives each segment of a speaker-blind model the diarizer slot most active over its span (online:
causally).

\paragraph{Metrics.} Whole meetings are scored by \textbf{tcpBLEU}, the translation analogue of
tcpWER~\citep{neumann2023meeteval}: output slots are matched to reference speakers per meeting by
chrF~\citep{popovic2015chrf}, and each output segment joins the reference unit of its matched speaker that it overlaps
within a 2\,s collar (Appendix~\ref{app:metrics}). BLEU is corpus-level sacreBLEU~\citep{post2018call} (13a
tokenisation for English, zh for Chinese). \textbf{ASR-BLEU} is tcpBLEU of the speech transcribed by
Whisper-large-v3~\citep{radford2023robust} (English) or Paraformer~\citep{gao2022paraformer} (Chinese), averaged over
two TTS seeds\ifMetricsV{}; COMET~\citep{rei2022comet} and a second recogniser are in Appendix~\ref{app:metricsv11}\fi.
\textbf{Latency} is measured per speaker stream from audio and playback time, without computation. Conventional speech
LAAL~\citep{papi2022over} charges every word of a block when the block starts playing (\emph{LAAL$_\text{block}$});
\emph{LAAL$_\text{spread}$} spreads a block's words over its playback, as Eq.~\eqref{eq:lat} does. We also report
\emph{speech start} (first heard word of a unit) and \emph{EndOffset} (unit end to the end of its last heard word);
all are defined in Appendix~\ref{app:chunk}.

\paragraph{Overlap-aware evaluation.}
\label{sec:eval-overlap}
A tcpBLEU unit is \emph{overlapped} when another speaker talks during at least 20\% of it. \emph{Concurrent-speaker
recall} (CR) takes every pair of utterances of two speakers that overlap by at least 0.5\,s and asks whether both
received text attributed to their own speaker within 1\,s.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Paired tests are over meetings (exact sign-flip test, 95\% meeting bootstrap, both TTS seeds
pooled). Confirmatory contrasts were fixed in internal analysis plans before test decoding (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg})
and Holm-corrected: \sys{} $-$ B over twelve BLEU tests (Table~\ref{tab:sig}), \sys{} $-$ R3$^\prime$ over six; other
differences are exploratory.
