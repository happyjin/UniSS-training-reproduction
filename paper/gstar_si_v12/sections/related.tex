\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Simultaneous speech translation.} Wait-$k$~\citep{ma2019stacl} writes after a fixed delay;
LocalAgreement~\citep{liu2020low,polak2022cuni,machacek2023turning} commits the prefix two consecutive hypotheses agree
on; learned policies emit a wait token~\citep{koshkin2024transllama} or read the decoder's attention to the
audio~\citep{papi2023edatt,papi2024streamatt}. Latency is measured with SimulEval~\citep{ma2020simuleval} and
LAAL~\citep{papi2022over}. SimulS2ST systems
keep one speaker's voice~\citep{zhang2024streamspeech,seamless2023,labiausse2025hibiki,cheng2025liveinterpret,he2026simuls2st,deng2025simuls2s}.

\paragraph{Latent timing and structured prediction.} Monotonic attention marginalises over write positions
(MILk;~\citealp{arivazhagan2019milk}), also per attention head (MMA;~\citealp{ma2020mma}) and for streaming speech
(EMMA, the policy of SeamlessStreaming;~\citealp{ma2023emma}); the Hidden Markov Transformer treats the moment a target
token starts as a hidden state and marginalises over it with an HMM~\citep{zhang2023hmt}; Hibiki places each target
word after the source that makes it predictable~\citep{labiausse2025hibiki}. All of these time the \emph{text}: their
latent variable is how much source has been read before a target token is written, and they are trained by the
likelihood of the target. Playback alignment times the \emph{speech} of text that is already fixed: its state is the
queue of synthesised speech in each speaker's stream, its transitions are deterministic queue dynamics rather than
learned, its potentials score renderings by intelligibility and by the delay at which a listener hears each word, and
it trains the speech generator, not the translation policy. Linear-chain CRFs~\citep{lafferty2001crf} with exact
forward--backward inference are standard for sequence labelling; posterior regularisation constrains expectations under
a latent posterior by a KL projection~\citep{ganchev2010posterior}, which we use to bound the expected delay at which
synthesised words are heard. Automatic dubbing controls the length of the translation~\citep{lakew2022isometric}; we
instead choose among renderings of a fixed text by their effect on the speaker's backlog.

\paragraph{Multi-speaker recognition and translation.} Serialised output training transcribes overlapping speakers
in order~\citep{kanda2020sot,kanda2021saasr}, also in streaming~\citep{kanda2022tsot} and for joint recognition and
translation~\citep{papi2023tsot}; streaming diarizers keep speaker order over long
recordings~\citep{park2024sortformer,medennikov2025streaming}, and diarization can condition a
recogniser~\citep{polok2026dicow,huo2026tagspeech} or be interleaved with a speech LLM's audio tokens
(G-STAR;~\citealp{peng2026gstar}), which we adopt. STAC-ST translates two-party calls offline~\citep{zuluaga2023stac};
DiariST~\citep{yang2024diarist} and X-Translator~\citep{zhao2026xtranslator} attribute streaming text translation to
speakers. Voice-cloned translation lowers listeners' workload in online meetings~\citep{ma2026samevoice}.
